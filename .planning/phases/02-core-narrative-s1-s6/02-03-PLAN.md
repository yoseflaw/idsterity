## Phase Goal

**As a** visitor, **I want to** see the APBN deficit and GDP government-consumption irony rendered as bilingual isometric coin-stack visuals across S2 and S3, **so that I** feel the numbers viscerally instead of reading them.

<objective>
Build the two macro-economic chart components for S2 and S3 and wire them into App.svelte as their own scrolly sections. Both share the isometric coin-stack pattern from UI-SPEC: each data point renders as a stack of CSS-isometric ellipses; stack height maps to value; active stack matches the scroll step. S2 has 3 stacks (3 steps), S3 has 5 quarters and 4 steps (step 2 spans Q3+Q4 per UI-SPEC). The S3 negative quarter (Q1 2025, -3.24%) renders as a coin pit below the baseline using rgba derivation of --red.

Purpose: Deliver the second and third vertical slices of the opening arc - the macroeconomic context that makes the procurement narrative (S5/S6) hit harder. Establish the dedicated-chart-per-section pattern (D-08) before the more complex InstitutionsChart (Plan 05).
Output: Two new Svelte components (DeficitChart.svelte, GDPChart.svelte) and updates to App.svelte adding both sections with sticky chart + scroll steps. Source citation links appear in each step card matching UI-SPEC.
</objective>
