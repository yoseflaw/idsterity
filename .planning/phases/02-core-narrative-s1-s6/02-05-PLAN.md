## Phase Goal

**As a** visitor, **I want to** see top-30 institutions stacked-bar by total pagu, then watch the chart re-sort to high-inappropriate-only ranking with a smooth animated transition, **so that I** experience the irony of who the biggest spenders are versus who actually has the most flagged spending.

Plan 02 already extended lembaga-totals.json with lowPagu/medPagu/highPagu fields so segment widths are honest. The BarChart.svelte component from the POC was deleted in Phase 1 (D-09); this plan creates its replacement.

Purpose: Deliver the emotional payoff of the opening arc. The S5 -> S6 re-sort is the visual punchline that converts the macro irony (S2/S3) into a specific procurement-level pointer.
Output: A new InstitutionsChart.svelte component and the final S5+S6 scrolly section in App.svelte. After this plan, Phase 2 is functionally complete pending end-to-end verification.
</objective>
